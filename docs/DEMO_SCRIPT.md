# Demo script (about 2 minutes)

Record the screen of your phone or a narrow browser window at https://wingu-buddy.vercel.app. Speak slowly. Say these points in your own words.

1. **(10 s) Hook.** "When I studied for AZ-900, I understood the ideas but still picked the wrong answers. Wingu Buddy fixes that."
2. **(30 s) Ask.** Tap the starter *Availability zones vs region pairs*. While it streams, point at the six parts: one line, simple explanation, **African example**, **where the example breaks**, **on the exam**, **your turn**.
3. **(15 s) Your turn.** Type a wrong answer to the question (for example "Two"). Show that the buddy says "Not quite" and explains.
4. **(15 s) Kiswahili.** Switch the language to Kiswahili and ask *Azure Key Vault inafanya kazi gani?* Note that product names stay in English, as on the exam.
5. **(25 s) Practice.** Open Practice, then Domain 2. Answer one question wrong on purpose, then tap **Why was I wrong? Ask Wingu Buddy**.
6. **(10 s) Progress.** Show the readiness bar weighted by the official domain weights, and the "Focus next" topic.
7. **(10 s) Offline.** Say: "No data or no AI credit? It falls back to 32 verified offline cards in English and Kiswahili."
8. **(5 s) Close.** "Unofficial, our own questions, open source. Asante!"

If the live AI is not switched on when you record, skip steps 3 and the streaming part of 2, and show the offline cards instead.
